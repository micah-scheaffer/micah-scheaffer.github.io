Hardware Engineering

8" Photogrammetry Drone

Designed a full-stack autonomous drone built for indoor 3D scanning. All powered on a custom fabricated battery pack with a power regulator. The system uses a Jetson Orin Nano for onboard compute, with a PX4 flight controller with integrated IMUs, stereo VSLAM cameras, RPLidar, and supporting sensors. The entire drone system runs on ROS2 locally, and exports data through Foxglove and Tailscale.

PX4 + ROS2 Edge Computing Systems Design Mechanical Design CAD Modeling

3.5" FPV Freestyle Drone

Realized that after a decade of competing in robotics I had very little knowlege about drones. Decided to buld a compact 3.5", sub-250g drone to learn about how drones work. Selected all the system components after in depth research and built the entire drone on a budget under $200. Gets up to 7 min of flight time and is capable of aerobatic maneuvers.
